# LiveScrollMatcher contract

`LiveScrollMatcher.match(previous:next:axis:configuration:)` compares two captured `ScrollFrame` luminance buffers. It does not require identical consecutive captures, retain images, create a composite, or mutate an accepted anchor. The legacy `ScrollStitcher` is unchanged.

- `displacement`: signed integer viewport motion; positive means newly revealed down/right content. `isStationary` / displacement zero is a successful no-op. Do not install that candidate as the accepted image or infer/lock new geometry from it.
- `contentBounds.rect`: the same half-open viewport rectangle in both inputs; `.insets` supplies explicit top/left/bottom/right values. Moving results include stationary-boundary confidence. Stationary results deliberately have bounds confidence zero.
- `previousOverlap` / `nextOverlap`: corresponding half-open pixel rectangles, useful for append/revisit decisions. Their origins differ by the signed displacement.
- `quality`: distributed textured support, coarse uniqueness gap, exact full-pixel verification count, inlier luminance error, exact count/fraction of pixels above tolerance, conservative mismatch bounding area and confidence.
- `mismatchRegions`: up to four connected-component boxes in the NEXT capture's coordinates. Pixel counts are exact and boxes enclose observed bad pixels; boxes can contain good pixels too. These are evidence, not an instruction to erase, replace or synthesize pixels.

After the first accepted moving match, set `Configuration.lockedContentBounds` to that result's `.contentBounds.rect`. The locked rectangle must cover the full cross-axis width/height and obey fixed-edge limits. Subsequent motion re-verifies stationary edges. Keep the previous accepted frame, immutable disk strips, bounds and preview unchanged when matching or the downstream disk transaction fails. This stateless module does not enforce a caller's transaction or output/disk quota.

Default bounds: minimum overlap 30%; maximum searched displacement 2,048 pixels (configuration hard cap 4,096); at most 18% per inferred fixed edge and 25% combined; at most 6% mismatched overlap pixels, 18% total mismatch-box area, four mismatch components. Any component spanning more than 55% of the overlap length, 60% of its cross-axis size, or 12% of its area is rejected. Broad static sidebars and large changes cannot become ignored animation regions.

Every signed integer displacement competes, including zero. Coarse scoring uses at most 1,200 spatial samples per offset. Positive matching evidence requires at least 12 textured supporting tiles spread over at least five of eight along bands, three of six cross bands, and all four quadrants. More than eight plausible finalists fails closed. At most eight finalists receive full-pixel verification, bounded by `ScrollFrame.maximumPixels`; cancellation is checked during search and verification. Worst-case native latency has not been measured in this checkout and must be qualified on the intended machine.

Fixed bands are inferred only along the chosen scroll axis. Stationary texture or a broad contrasting edge is required; recovered motion must contradict the fixed-edge hypothesis's alternative. Coincidental flat paragraph whitespace stays inside the verified content rectangle. Pairwise evidence cannot prove arbitrary web-page semantics. Broad sidebar layouts, moving sticky controls, large animation, repeated-only content, and insufficient distributed texture may reject and require a narrower selection or backscroll. This module sees luminance only and cannot detect isoluminant color-only replacements; the capture/session layer must avoid claiming exact-color equivalence.

## Verification boundary

Eighteen new XCTest cases are authored in `Tests/PicShotCoreTests/LiveScrollMatcherTests.swift`. Swift is absent in this cloud checkout, so these tests were not compiled or run here. Independent NumPy/Pillow behavioral probes checked both signed directions, one-pixel motion, noisy changing frames, fixed bars, outlier components, replacement/sidebar/periodic-content rejection and a rendered text page with a fixed 36-pixel toolbar. This is supplemental algorithm evidence, not native Swift or app evidence. The owning integration must run the new XCTest suite and the real native scroll demo, and measure release-build matching latency/memory at target capture sizes.
